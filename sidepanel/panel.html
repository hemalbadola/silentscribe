Transcript Cleanup

        

          After transcribing, send the transcript to your notes provider to fix
          stutters, repetitions and obvious mis-hearings. Off by default: it
          costs one extra request per recording, and it sends the transcript
          automatically rather than only when you ask for notes.
        

        
        
      


      

        
Live Transcript

        

          Show roughly what is being said while recording. This runs speech
          recognition during capture, which competes with the recording for CPU,
          so it is off by default. The full transcript is always produced after
          the recording ends, whether this is on or not.
